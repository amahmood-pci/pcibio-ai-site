IHC Control Panels

Precision cell line
control panels

TriControl™, QuadControl™, and DualControl™ panels enrich biomarker antibody optimization and decrease time for IHC assessment. Characterized cell line plugs provide reproducible expression benchmarks across null, wild-type, and overexpression patterns.

3

TriControl™

Three-cell-line IHC panel

Three cell lines presenting null (mutant), wild-type, and diffuse (overexpressed) expression patterns. Built for antibody optimization and assay development across triple-biomarker IHC panels including p53 and p16.

Null · Wild-type · Overexpressed
4

QuadControl™

Four-cell-line IHC panel

Four cell lines covering null, low-normal, high-normal, and overexpression. The MMR QuadControl™ consolidates MLH1, MSH2, MSH6, and PMS2 mismatch repair proteins in a single, high-throughput FFPE block.

MLH1 · MSH2 · MSH6 · PMS2
2

DualControl™

Two-cell-line IHC panel

One or two cell lines contrasting in expression levels to improve assessment capabilities. Available in wildtype/knockout and overexpression variants, including MSH6, PDL1, PTEN, BRAF V600E, p53, and p16 configurations.

Wildtype · Knockout
